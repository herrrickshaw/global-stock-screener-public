**For all Tier 2+ companies:**

   - "Raising capex guidance" → Score should rise
   - "Cutting capex" → Score should fall
   - Watch earnings call commentary

✅ **Free Cash Flow Trend**
   - FCF declining while capex rising → Yellow flag
   - FCF positive despite high capex → Green light

✅ **Operating Income Growth**
   - OI growing faster than capex → ROI materializing
   - OI flat/declining despite capex → Red flag

✅ **Debt Issuances**
   - New debt + capex announcement → Expected
   - Debt repayment + high capex → Distressed

✅ **Tier Movements**
   - Moves from Tier 2 → Tier 1 → Scoring improving (good)
   - Moves from Tier 2 → Tier 3/4 → Deterioration (risky)
